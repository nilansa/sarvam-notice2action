Office of the District Revenue Officer. For income certificate attestation, applicants must first collect Form IC-12 at the Reception Desk, Ground Floor. Fill the form and attach photocopies of Aadhaar and income proof. Applicants under 18 must also attach a parent/guardian ID. Pay the stated service fee at the Cash Counter, First Floor (the amount is not specified in this notice), retain the receipt, then submit the filled form with receipt at Counter 5, Second Floor between 10 AM and 2 PM on working days. Staff will issue an acknowledgment slip. If any photocopy is missing, the application will be returned. Processing time and certificate collection date will be communicated on the acknowledgment.
